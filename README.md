<h2>Description</h2>
I competed in a nationwide robotics competition at Carnegie Mellon University Qatar and achieved a top-four position out of 20+ schools.
I Was the team leader for one of the two robots we manufactured and programmed as part of an extracurricular project along with other students from the A-level computer science class. I Leveraged motors, servos, digital and analogue sensors, as well as metal plates and Wallaby programming platform to design and programme robot as team leader and main programmer for “Big-Bot”.
